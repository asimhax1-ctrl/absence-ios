# Present Absence (حضور الغياب)

A lecture you attend leaves no trace. A lecture you miss leaves a hole. This movement treats a semester as a field of small marks, one for every hour of class, and draws the identity only through the places where marks are missing. The emblem never draws the letter. The letter appears as the absence the field leaves around it.

The field is grown with Poisson-disc sampling, so marks keep an even, organic spacing, the way students fill a lecture hall without ever forming a grid. A single Arabic form is rasterised into an invisible mask: the letter «غ», or the whole word «غياب». Every mark that falls inside it is removed. A chamfer distance transform measures how far each surviving mark sits from that void, and marks shrink smoothly as they approach it. The edge reads like attendance thinning out near a deadline. This algorithm should feel meticulously crafted: every spacing ratio and falloff curve tuned the way a master type designer tunes optical overshoot.

Arabic letters carry their dots (nuqat) as separate bodies, and the algorithm respects that. A connected-component pass splits the mask into the letter bodies and the small islands of the dots. The bodies become absence. The dots stay presence: they are drawn back as solid, perfectly round discs, the only filled shapes in the whole composition. The result is the inverse of the usual relation: what is normally a small detail becomes the strongest mark on the icon. The reversal is intentional and precisely balanced, the product of deep computational care.

Variation comes from the seed, not from decoration. Low-frequency noise gently modulates mark size, so the field breathes without becoming noisy. In the stroke modes, the same noise sets each tick's direction, so the marks read like a hand-ticked register. Every seed gives a new arrangement of the same idea, like successive prints from one plate, and each one should look as though it was refined over countless iterations rather than randomly generated.

The palette is a single ink on a single ground, either black on white or white on black. Nothing else is needed: an iPhone icon at 60 points is judged by its silhouette. The superellipse container follows the continuous curvature of iOS icons, and the field stays inset from its edge so the mark holds at any size, from the home screen down to the Settings list. This is a master-level piece of parametric identity in which restraint is the most important parameter.
